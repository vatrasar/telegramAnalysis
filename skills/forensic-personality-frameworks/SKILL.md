# Forensic Personality Frameworks & Diagnostic Taxonomies

This skill defines the empirical diagnostic criteria, conversational markers, and psychological classification rubrics used to evaluate Attachment Style, Big Five (OCEAN) traits, defense mechanisms, and narrative archetypes from longitudinal chat logs.

---

## 1. Overview

While basic profiling principles (cross-examining words vs. actions) are maintained in core rules, deep psychological diagnosis requires detailed empirical taxonomies to map chat behaviors to established psychodynamic frameworks.

This skill equips the agent with:
1. **The Big Five (OCEAN) Observational Profiling Rubric:** Detailed linguistic and behavioral markers in chat logs for Low, Moderate, and High poles of all 5 traits.
2. **Attachment Theory Diagnostic Taxonomy:** Classification criteria for all 4 attachment styles and specific deactivating intimacy strategies.
3. **Narrative Self-Presentation Archetypes:** Classifications for recurring storytelling lenses (Hero, Victim, Comedian, Detached Observer).
4. **Trait Triangulation Framework:** Combining Big Five and Attachment dynamics to illuminate core defenses.

---

## 2. When to Use

Activate this skill whenever:
- Formulating the **Attachment Style Diagnosis** for a subject.
- Rating the subject across the **Big Five (OCEAN)** spectrums (Low / Moderate / High) with concrete conversational evidence.
- Investigating intimacy sabotage, relational deactivating maneuvers, or protest behaviors.
- Identifying narrative self-presentation archetypes in personal anecdotes.
- Assembling Section 5 and Section 6 of the personality dossier.

---

## 3. Attachment Theory & Relational Dynamics

Evaluate the subject against the 4 empirical attachment styles based on behavioral reactions to closeness, conflict, and separation:

### 1. Secure Attachment (Bezpieczny)
- **Chat Markers:** Direct, vulnerable communication without defensive masking; comfort with emotional reciprocity; resolves conflict constructively without stonewalling or catastrophic anxiety; accepts partner's independence without feeling abandoned.

### 2. Anxious-Preoccupied Attachment (Lękowo-Zaabsorbowany)
- **Chat Markers:** Hyper-activating strategies; chronic reassurance-seeking; hyper-sensitivity to delayed responses or silence gaps; interprets brief messages as catastrophic rejection; message bombarding during emotional distress; difficulty self-soothing without relational validation.

### 3. Dismissive-Avoidant Attachment (Unikająco-Oddalający)
- **Chat Markers:** Deactivating strategies; compulsive self-reliance (*"I don't need anyone"*); intellectualizes emotional discussions; views vulnerability as weakness; shuts down or stonewalls during interpersonal tension; devalues the partner when emotional intimacy deepens.

### 4. Fearful-Avoidant Attachment / Disorganized (Lękowo-Unikający)
- **Chat Markers:** Volatile push-pull dynamics; intense craving for deep emotional intimacy paired with acute terror of engulfment and betrayal; presents a guarded, cynical exterior until deep trust is formed, followed by sudden vulnerability, panic, and abrupt retreat; high sensitivity to perceived shifts in partner warmth.

### Deactivating Strategies & Intimacy Sabotage:
Actively probe the archive for these specific intimacy-limiting defenses:
- **Checklist Perfectionism:** Compulsively identifying minor flaws in prospective or present partners to justify terminating deepening bonds.
- **Phantom Ex / Unattainable Ideal:** Fixating on a distant, idealized past partner or unavailable crush to avoid engaging with real, present partners.
- **Geographic & Relational Buffers:** Preferentially choosing long-distance connections, emotionally unavailable partners, or digital-only relationships to prevent physical or emotional vulnerability.
- **Secret Relational Exit Doors:** Maintaining financial secrecy, alternate living plans, or private backup options to ensure an escape route is always accessible.

## 4. Big Five Personality Model (OCEAN - Observational Profiling)

Evaluate the subject across all 5 spectrums (Low / Moderate / High). Rate empirically based on longitudinal conversational patterns:

### 1. Openness to Experience (Otwartość na doświadczenie)
- **High:** Deep intellectual curiosity, philosophical exploration, fascination with abstract concepts and unconventional theories, exploratory travel (seeking cultural novelty and foreign horizons), enthusiastic experimentation with AI and cutting-edge tech, rich metaphorical vocabulary, aesthetic appreciation.
- **Low:** Pragmatic focus on immediate concrete reality, strict adherence to conventional routines, homebody orientation avoiding travel or novelty, dismissal of abstract speculation and tech disruptions, literal and functional communication style.

### 2. Conscientiousness (Sumienność)
- **High:** High structural discipline, strong work ethic ("pracuś", diligence, taking ownership), systematic execution of declared plans over time (high follow-through, milestones achieved), regular physical training routine, domestic cleanliness and orderliness (intolerance of clutter), disciplined financial management (budgeting, frugality, long-term saving), clean message formatting (bullet points, clear paragraphs, syntactic precision).
- **Low:** Spontaneous stream-of-consciousness bursts, disorganization, chronic procrastination or slacking ("obijanie się"), recurring ambitious declarations without real-world execution (phantom plans, wishful thinking, abandoned projects), sedentary physical habits, high tolerance for domestic clutter, impulsive spending or lack of financial planning, abandoning threads mid-thought.

### 3. Extraversion (Ekstrawersja)
- **High:** High conversational initiative, low latency in reconnecting after gaps (`--gaps`), external processing (thinking out loud via message bursts), expressive emotional valence, active desire for social contact, frequent offline meetups with friends, preference for group activities, team sports, and high-energy gatherings.
- **Low (Introversion):** Solitary recharge, deliberate paragraph synthesis, comfort with prolonged silence, high selectivity in dialogue partners, rare or curated offline meetups, conversational energy depleting during group chatter, preference for solo hobbies, solo fitness (running, gym, solo training), and solitary retreats.

### 4. Agreeableness (Ugodowość)
- **High:** Empathic validation, active de-escalation of interpersonal tension, prosocial cooperation, natural empathy and warmth toward animals and vulnerable beings, altruism and genuine joy in helping others, collaborative team-player mindset, willingness to accommodate and soothe, avoidance of harsh friction.
- **Low:** Argumentative debate stance, intellectual skepticism, blunt confrontation, indifference or instrumental stance toward others/animals, lone-wolf mentality (resisting team dependence), conditional or reluctant helping, low fear of relational rupture, competitive drive in ideological clashes, skepticism of other people's motives.

### 5. Neuroticism / Emotional Stability (Neurotyczność / Stabilność emocjonalna)
- **High Neuroticism (Low Stability):** Heightened emotional volatility, rumination, acute sensitivity to perceived slights, vulnerability decompensation during late-night fatigue ("Nighttime Truth"), financial anxiety and catastrophic money fears, inability to relax on vacations/holidays, chronic anxiety spikes on anomalous peak days (`--burning-days`).
- **Low Neuroticism (High Stability):** Emotional poise under pressure, stoic baseline affect, resilient recovery from conflict, resistance to spiral thinking, even-tempered interaction style, relaxed attitude toward uncertainties.

## 5. Narrative Self-Presentation Archetypes

Identify recurring archetypes and storytelling lenses in personal anecdotes:

1. **The Hero:** High agency, conquering systemic odds, lone fighter overcoming widespread incompetence, personal sacrifices made for a greater mission.
2. **The Victim:** External locus of control, chronically misunderstood, unappreciated, or actively sabotaged by bosses, ex-partners, or unfair systems.
3. **The Comedian:** Converting acute trauma, embarrassment, and emotional pain into stand-up routines, cynical memes, and self-deprecating wit.
4. **The Detached Observer:** A clinical, alien-like spectator studying human absurdity from an ivory tower, intellectualizing personal feelings as abstract sociological phenomena.

---

## 6. Trait Triangulation & Diagnostic Synthesis

Cross-examine how Big Five traits interact with Attachment Style and relational defenses:
- **Introversion + Low Agreeableness + High Openness:** Produces an intellectualized, detached critic who challenges social orthodoxies while maintaining an ivory tower buffer.
- **High Neuroticism + Fearful-Avoidant Attachment:** Drives intense push-pull volatility, late-night vulnerability decompensation ("Nighttime Truth"), and hyper-vigilance toward partner withdrawal.
- **High Conscientiousness + Low Agreeableness:** Manifests as a demanding perfectionist ("pracuś") with low tolerance for colleagues' slackness, frequently clashing with authority over incompetence.

Before completing the personality evaluation, verify:

- [ ] Were deactivating strategies and relational buffers identified and grounded in quotes?
- [ ] Did the analysis avoid all pop-psychology typologies (MBTI, Enneagram, etc.)?
