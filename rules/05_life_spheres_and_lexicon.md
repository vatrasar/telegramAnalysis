# Life Spheres Evidentiary Standards & Investigation Protocol

These rules define the evidentiary threshold, omission protocol, and activation gate for investigating the foundational spheres of life in chat archives.

---

## 1. Evidentiary Threshold & Omission Protocol

The life spheres (Social Matrix, Professional/Finances, Worldview/Tech, Family/Domestic, Romance, Leisure/Animals) represent an **inquiry guide, NOT a mandatory list of headers to generate**:
1. **Evidence-Driven Inclusion:** If targeted queries (`--semantic`, keyword searches `-s`) yield substantive evidence on a topic, synthesize that dimension into the report.
2. **Strict Omission of Unaddressed Topics (True Non-Existence):** If a specific lifestyle dimension was **never mentioned or introduced anywhere in the chat archive**, completely omit that sub-topic from the report.
3. **Absolute Prohibition on Placeholder Disclaimers:** NEVER generate empty sections or negative boilerplate such as *"Brak informacji o sporcie"*, *"Temat ten nie był poruszany w rozmowie"*, or *"No information available in the archive"*.
4. **Stimulus Exposure & Disinterest as an Analytical Finding:**
   - When an interlocutor introduces any topic, activity, or suggestion and the subject exhibits disinterest, changes the subject, or avoids engaging:
   - This should not be treated merely as a complete absence of information, but the agent must also avoid forcing far-fetched or over-pathologized interpretations.
   - If relevant to the sphere, this disinterest can be recorded as a valid observation of personal priorities or boundaries.

---

## 2. Mandatory Life Spheres Skill Activation Gate

To preserve system context, detailed forensic diagnostic questions, concrete behavioral probing criteria across all life spheres, and bilingual search lexicons are maintained in specialized skills:

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Activation Requirement:**
> When conducting Phase 4 of an investigation (auditing lifestyle habits, work ethic, unfulfilled ambitions, friendships, spending psychology, domestic order, hobbies, or technology stance), the agent **MUST activate the `life-spheres-investigation` skill**.
>
> The skill provides:
> - Concrete forensic probing criteria across all 6 core life spheres.
> - Detailed operational checklists and behavioral probing questions.
> - The Master Bilingual Probing Lexicon (Polish FTS5 keywords and English semantic vector queries).
> - Concrete CLI recipes for `query_chat.py` and step-by-step verification procedures.
