# Life Spheres Evidentiary Standards & Investigation Protocol

These rules define the evidentiary threshold, omission protocol, and activation gate for investigating the foundational spheres of life in chat archives.

---

## 1. Evidentiary Threshold, Stimulus Rejection & Omission Protocol

The life spheres (Social Matrix, Professional/Finances, Worldview/Tech, Family/Domestic, Romance, Leisure/Animals) represent an **inquiry guide, NOT a mandatory list of headers to generate**:
1. **Evidence-Driven Inclusion:** If targeted queries (`--semantic`, keyword searches `-s`) yield substantive evidence on a topic, synthesize that dimension into the report.
2. **Strict Omission of Unaddressed Topics (True Non-Existence):** If a specific lifestyle dimension (e.g. extreme sports, pet ownership, specific travel experiences, domestic cleaning habits) was **never mentioned or introduced anywhere in the chat archive**, completely omit that sub-topic from the report.
3. **Absolute Prohibition on Placeholder Disclaimers:** NEVER generate empty sections or negative boilerplate such as *"Brak informacji o sporcie"*, *"Temat ten nie był poruszany w rozmowie"*, or *"No information available in the archive"*.
4. **Stimulus Rejection & Apathy as Positive Empirical Evidence:**
   - When an interlocutor introduces a topic (e.g. encouraging the subject to test a modern digital tool or assistant, discussing political developments, proposing athletic activities, or raising societal debates) and the subject exhibits disinterest, passive avoidance, apathy, refusal to engage, or evasive deflection:
   - This must **NEVER** be treated as a lack of evidence. It is a verifiable behavioral indicator of the subject's cognitive openness, technological posture, or psychological defenses, and **MUST be synthesized** into the corresponding life sphere.
5. **Political & Civic Stance Scope (Beyond Formal Doctrine):**
   - Political orientation in longitudinal personal chats is rarely expressed through formal party affiliations or academic political philosophy.
   - The agent MUST capture concrete political postures revealed in the archive:
     - Sympathies or antipathies toward specific political figures, movements, or candidate types.
     - Psychological motivations behind civic and voting behavior (e.g. voting driven by personal empathy/pity, peer/family expectations, protest, or indifference).
     - Geopolitical alignments and cultural/religious solidarity in international conflicts.
     - Interpersonal boundaries and tolerance when interlocutors express conflicting political or ideological stances (e.g. conditional tolerance vs. ideological friction).
