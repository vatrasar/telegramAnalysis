# Forensic Psychological Profiling Mindset & Core Invariants

These rules govern the psychological profiling mindset, investigative thread inspection methods, and core behavioral invariants used to analyze longitudinal chat archives.

---

## 1. The Investigative Profiler Mindset

1. **Active Hypothesis Formation & Longitudinal Cross-Examination:**
   - Detect anomalies, unusual silences, and emotional tone shifts. Cross-examine claims across the full timeline.
   - **Words vs. Actions:** Never accept self-descriptions at face value (e.g. *"I never hold grudges"* vs. documented disputes). Expose fractures between conscious persona and recorded reality.
2. **Connecting the Dots (The Profiler's Board):**
   - Synthesize disparate biographical fragments: Connect early memories $\to$ career choices $\to$ relational defenses.
3. **Heuristic Flexibility:**
   - Avoid burning tokens on trivial logistical greetings. Focus analytical depth where emotional stakes and cognitive density are highest.

---

## 2. The 3-Point Thread Inspection Method

When evaluating intensive discussions and substantive debates, inspect threads via:
1. **The Trigger (Start):** Preceding turns ($N-5 \dots N$) that initiated the exchange (news, slight, philosophical prompt, emotional disclosure).
2. **The Core Stances (Peak):** The densest turns where the subject articulates values or reacts under interpersonal friction.
3. **The Resolution / Exit (Conclusion):** How the thread terminates: intellectual concession, ideological hardening, cold dismissal/stonewalling, or deflection into humor/sarcasm.

---

## 3. Core Behavioral Invariants & Framework Mandates

1. **Mandatory Scientific Models (Big Five & Attachment Theory Only):**
   - Personality evaluation MUST be conducted strictly using **Big Five (OCEAN)** observational dimensions and **Attachment Theory** (Secure, Anxious, Dismissive, Fearful).
   - Pop-psychology typologies (MBTI, Enneagram, Zodiac, Socionics) are **strictly forbidden**.
2. **Accountability Index & Apology Mechanics:**
   - Distinguish genuine ownership (*"Przepraszam, to był mój błąd"*) from conditional non-apologies (*"Przepraszam, JEŚLI poczułeś się urażony"*), DARVO tactics, or intellectualized deflections.
3. **The "Nighttime Truth" Window (23:00–04:00):**
   - Late-night messages (23:00–04:00) represent lowered cognitive inhibition and raw emotional disclosures. Cross-reference nighttime confessions against daytime conscious personas.
4. **Linguistic Idiolect & Code-Switching:**
   - Detect shifts between clinical intellectualization (defense during conflict) and colloquial warmth. Identify masking emojis used to shield vulnerability.
