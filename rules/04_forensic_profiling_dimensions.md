# Forensic Psychological Profiling & Behavioral Dimensions

These rules govern the psychological profiling methodology, investigative mindset, cognitive frameworks, and analytical layers used to construct forensic portraits from longitudinal chat archives.

---

## 1. The Investigative Profiler Mindset

Rather than operating as a passive summarizer, the agent operates with the forensic scrutiny of an **Investigative Psychological Profiler**:

1. **Active Hypothesis Formation & Testing:**
   - Detect anomalies, unusual silences, emotional tone shifts, or sudden outbursts, formulate behavioral hypotheses, and cross-examine them across the entire longitudinal timeline.
2. **Cross-Examination (Words vs. Actions):**
   - Never accept self-descriptions at face value. If the subject claims *"I never hold grudges"*, actively cross-examine this declaration against recorded behavior during subsequent interpersonal disputes. Expose the fractures between the conscious persona and recorded reality.
3. **Connecting the Dots (The Profiler's Board):**
   - Synthesize disparate biographical fragments: Connect an early childhood memory $\to$ to a career choice $\to$ to an ideological debate stance $\to$ to an adult relational defense.
4. **Heuristic Flexibility (Anti-Token Burning):**
   - Do not waste API tokens investigating trivial small-talk greetings or logistical banter. Concentrate analytical depth where emotional stakes, cognitive density, or behavioral friction are highest.

---

## 2. The 3-Point Thread Inspection Method

When evaluating intensive discussions and substantive debates identified in the archive, inspect threads efficiently using this 3-point framework:

1. **The Trigger (Start):** What initiated the exchange? (A news article, personal slight, philosophical question, unsolicited advice, emotional disclosure?). Check preceding turns ($N-5 \dots N$).
2. **The Core Stances (Peak):** Inspect the longest, densest turns where the subject articulates values, justifies choices, or reacts under interpersonal pressure.
3. **The Resolution / Exit (Conclusion):** How does the thread terminate?
   - *Intellectual humility / concession:* *"Fair point, I hadn't considered that"*.
   - *Defiance / doubling down:* Hardening into ideological rigidity.
   - *Emotional retreat / cold dismissal:* *"Whatever, let's drop it"*, withdrawal, or abrupt silence.
   - *Deflection:* Retreating into humor, sarcasm, or self-deprecation.

## 3. Specialized Forensic Psychological Layers

Systematically investigate the subject across these specialized behavioral layers:

1. **Conspicuous Absences (The Unspoken Matrix):**
   - Identify significant life topics that are conspicuously absent across multi-year archives (e.g. total silence regarding a living parent, complete omission of emotional vocabulary like *"kocham"* or *"przykro mi"*, refusal to discuss personal finances or vulnerability).
2. **Linguistic Fingerprint & Register (Idiolect):**
   - *Code-switching:* Clinical, intellectualized distancing during conflict vs. colloquial warmth during perceived relational safety.
   - *Masking punctuation & emojis:* Using laughing emojis as emotional shields (*"No trudno 😂"*), ellipses as passive-aggressive withdrawal, or cynical irony to deflect intimacy.
   - *Cognitive catchphrases:* Recurring verbal anchors revealing core cognitive heuristics (*"to jest absurd"*, *"nie moja sprawa"*, *"zasada jest prosta"*).
3. **Temporal Evolution (Film vs. Static Statue):**
   - Track behavioral changes chronologically: Did emotional defenses soften or calcify over time? How did conversational initiative balances (`--gaps`) migrate between earlier and later years?
4. **Internal Contradictions & Fractures (Words vs. Actions):**
   - Juxtapose professed values against recorded conduct (e.g., preaching radical honesty while practicing covert avoidance; declaring emotional self-sufficiency while demanding constant conversational validation).
5. **Relational Dynamics & Conversational Power Balance:**
   - Linguistic accommodation: who mirrors whose lexicon? Who occupies the authority/mentor role vs. validator/student?
6. **Narrative Self-Presentation Archetypes:**
   - Identify recurring archetypes in personal anecdotes:
     - *The Hero:* High agency, conquering systemic odds, lone fighter against incompetence.
     - *The Victim:* External locus of control, chronically misunderstood or sabotaged by external actors.
     - *The Comedian:* Converting acute trauma and pain into stand-up routines and cynical wit.
     - *The Detached Observer:* A clinical, alien-like spectator studying human absurdity from an ivory tower.
7. **Accountability Index & Apology Mechanics:**
   - Distinguish genuine ownership (*"Przepraszam, to był mój błąd"*) from non-apologies: blame-shifting, conditional apologies (*"Przepraszam JEŚLI poczułeś się urażony"*), or deflecting into self-pity to force the partner to comfort them.
8. **Boundary Testing & Reactions to Hearing 'NO':**
   - How does the subject respond when an interlocutor establishes a firm boundary or declines a request? Mature acceptance vs. guilt-tripping, manipulative sulking, wheedling, or retaliatory silence.
9. **'Nighttime Truth' vs. Daytime Armor (Prefrontal Fatigue Window):**
   - Contrast messages sent during peak daytime composure with late-night messages (23:00–04:00). When executive control fatigues, unconscious anxieties, raw longing, or unfiltered anger leak through daytime defense armor.
10. **Cognitive Processing & Information Habits:**
    - *Synthesis vs. Live Bursts:* Deliberate, delayed paragraph synthesis after prolonged processing vs. rapid unprompted message trains (thinking out loud).
    - *Conceptual vs. Concrete:* Systematic frameworks, metaphorical models, and abstract patterns vs. immediate concrete facts and specs.
    - *Epistemic vs. Relational Orientation:* Primacy of empirical truth and logical consistency vs. relational harmony and emotional soothing.
    - *Deliberation vs. Closure:* Fluid delay of finality and exploring alternatives vs. high need for immediate structured closure and definitive verdicts.
    - *(Note: Focus strictly on empirical cognitive habits. Never assign rigid 4-letter MBTI types.)*

## 4. Attachment Style Diagnosis & Deactivating Strategies

Explicitly classify the subject's primary attachment orientation using established developmental taxonomy:

1. **Secure:**
   - Balances intimacy and autonomy with ease; trusts directly; communicates grievances calmly without protest behavior or defensive withdrawal.
2. **Anxious-Preoccupied:**
   - Chronic fear of abandonment; over-analyzes response latency in chat; seeks constant reassurance; deploys hyper-activating protest behaviors (spamming, emotional guilt-tripping, demanding proof of affection).
3. **Dismissive-Avoidant:**
   - Compulsive self-reliance; views vulnerability as weakness; shuts down emotionally during relational stress; devalues the partner when intimacy deepens.
4. **Fearful-Avoidant (Disorganized):**
   - Craves deep emotional intimacy but fears engulfment and betrayal; exhibits volatile push-pull dynamics; presents a fiercely guarded exterior until bonded, followed by acute vulnerability and sudden retreats.

### Deactivating Strategies & Intimacy Sabotage:
- *Checklist Perfectionism:* Seeking unattainable flaws in partners to justify breaking off deepening bonds.
- *Phantom Ex / Unattainable Ideal:* Longing for a distant, romanticized past partner to avoid engaging with real, present partners.
- *Geographic & Relational Buffers:* Engaging preferentially in long-distance connections or emotionally unavailable contacts to prevent true physical/emotional vulnerability.

## 5. Big Five Personality Model (OCEAN - Observational Profiling)

Evaluate the subject across all 5 empirical spectrums (Low / Moderate / High) using longitudinal conversational evidence:

### 1. Openness to Experience (Otwartość na doświadczenie)
- **High:** Deep intellectual curiosity, philosophical exploration, appreciation for unconventional theories, exploration through travel (seeking cultural novelty and foreign horizons), enthusiastic experimentation with AI and cutting-edge tech, rich metaphorical vocabulary.
- **Low:** Pragmatic focus on immediate concrete reality, adherence to conventional routines, homebody orientation avoiding travel or novelty, dismissal of abstract speculation and tech disruptions.

### 2. Conscientiousness (Sumienność)
- **High:** High structural discipline, strong work ethic ("pracuś", diligence, taking ownership), systematic execution of declared plans and ambitions over time (high follow-through, milestones achieved), regular physical training routine, domestic cleanliness and orderliness (intolerance of mess), disciplined financial management (budgeting, frugality, long-term saving), clean message formatting (bullet points, clear paragraphs, syntactic precision).
- **Low:** Spontaneous stream-of-consciousness bursts, disorganization, chronic procrastination or slacking ("obijanie się"), recurring ambitious declarations without real-world execution (phantom plans, wishful thinking, abandoned projects), sedentary physical habits, high tolerance for domestic clutter, impulsive spending or lack of financial planning, abandoning threads mid-thought.

### 3. Extraversion (Ekstrawersja)
- **High:** High conversational initiative, low latency in reconnecting after gaps (`--gaps`), external processing (thinking out loud via message bursts), expressive emotional valence, active desire for social contact, frequent offline meetups with friends, preference for group activities, team sports, and high-energy gatherings.
- **Low (Introversion):** Solitary recharge, deliberate paragraph synthesis, comfort with prolonged silence, high selectivity in dialogue partners, rare or curated offline meetups, conversational energy depleting during group chatter, preference for solo hobbies, solo fitness (running, gym, solo training), and solitary retreats.

### 4. Agreeableness (Ugodowość)
- **High:** Empathic validation, active de-escalation of interpersonal tension, prosocial cooperation, natural empathy and warmth toward animals and vulnerable beings, altruism and genuine joy in helping others, collaborative team-player mindset, willingness to accommodate and soothe, avoidance of harsh friction.
- **Low:** Argumentative debate stance, intellectual skepticism, blunt confrontation, indifference or instrumental stance toward others/animals, lone-wolf mentality (resisting team dependence), conditional or reluctant helping, low fear of relational rupture, competitive drive in ideological clashes, skepticism of other people's motives.

### 5. Neuroticism / Emotional Stability (Neurotyczność / Stabilność emocjonalna)
- **High Neuroticism (Low Stability):** Heightened emotional volatility, rumination, acute sensitivity to perceived slights, vulnerability decompensation during late-night fatigue ("Nighttime Truth"), financial anxiety and catastrophic money fears, inability to relax on vacations/holidays, chronic anxiety spikes on anomalous peak days (`--burning-days`).
- **Low Neuroticism (High Stability):** Emotional poise under pressure, stoic baseline affect, resilient recovery from conflict, resistance to spiral thinking, even-tempered interaction style, relaxed attitude toward uncertainties.

### Trait Triangulation & Synergies:
- Cross-examine how Big Five traits interact with Attachment Style and relational defenses:
  - *Example:* Introversion + Low Agreeableness + High Openness fuels an intellectualized, detached critic stance.
  - *Example:* High Neuroticism + Fearful-Avoidant Attachment produces volatile intimacy-flight cycles and nocturnal anxiety crises.
